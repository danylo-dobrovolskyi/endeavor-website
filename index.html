08 / Questions

Things founders ask.

If yours isn't here, write to us
Is the price per person?
Yes, we invite one founder per company. The tuition fee includes lodging (single-person rooms), meals, and course materials for the duration of the program.
Can I choose my accommodations and/or bring family?
It is the policy of the universities that all program participants stay in on-campus housing as designated by each program. This policy prohibits spouses or other family members from staying on-campus. This is to maintain a cohesive cohort experience dedicated to participants’ focused learning journey.
What if I need a visa letter?
Please email us for a visa letter to submit to your consulate at: evelina.holovanova@endeavor.org. In this email, please state your name as it appears on your passport, your passport number, and the address of the embassy you will submit this visa letter to.
What happens after I share interest?
We review every response on onrolling basis & come back to those who fit with the application form. Selected founders pay by 10 July. Programme runs 16 – 24 August.
How can I pay?
After being selected, we will provide a link for payment & registration.
