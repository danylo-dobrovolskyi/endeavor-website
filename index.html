08 / Questions

Things founders ask.

If yours isn't here, write to us
Is the price per person?
Yes – each founder pays for their own seat. Co-founders applying together each apply and pay separately.
Do I need a US visa? Do you help?
Most Ukrainian passport holders need a US B-1/B-2 visa. We provide invitation letters and supporting documentation once you're confirmed. Processing times vary by consulate – we recommend starting your application immediately after confirmation. Flights and personal travel are your responsibility, though we'll help coordinate group bookings if there's interest.
Can my company pay? Can you invoice?
Yes – we can invoice your company directly. Mention this when applications open and we'll set up the right paperwork.
What happens after I share interest?
We review every response. By mid-June, we come back to those who fit the brief with the application form. Selected founders confirm and pay by end of June. Visa, travel, and prep run through July. Programme runs 16 – 24 August.
Is what we share in the room confidential?
Yes – Chatham House. Founders speak openly. We don't make recordings or notes public.
Is there a +1 option?
To be confirmed – we're working through the details. If +1s are possible we'll share the policy when applications open.
