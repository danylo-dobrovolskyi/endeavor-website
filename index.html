04 / Questions

Things founders ask

If yours isn't here, write to us
Who is it for?
The program is designed for founders of high-growth tech companies with the ambition to become local, regional, and global champions.
What are the selection criteria?
Participants will be selected by a committee consisting of Endeavor leadership and Stanford program alumni. The committee will consider each founder’s background, company stage, and growth.

Founders of scale-ups and early applicants will be given priority during the review process.
What is included in the Stanford program cost?
The total price per seat of $10,000 includes lodging (single-person rooms), meals, and course materials for the duration of the program.
Can I choose my accommodations and/or bring family?
It is the policy of the universities that all program participants stay in on-campus housing as designated by each program. This policy prohibits spouses or other family members from staying on-campus. This is to maintain a cohesive cohort experience dedicated to participants’ focused learning journey.
What if I need a visa letter?
Please email us for a visa letter to submit to your consulate at: evelina.holovanova@endeavor.org. In this email, please state your name as it appears on your passport, your passport number, and the address of the embassy you will submit this visa letter to.
What happens after I share interest?
We review every response on a rolling basis & come back to those who fit with the application form. Selected founders pay by 10 July. Program runs 16 – 21 August.
How can I pay?
After being selected, we will provide a link for payment & registration.
